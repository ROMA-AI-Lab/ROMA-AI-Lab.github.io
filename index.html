ROMA Lab

ROMA Lab (Responsible Open-world Modeling and Agents) works at the frontier of data mining and graph-native intelligence — from nonlinear foundation theory and graph prompting to LLM agents and multimodal reasoning, with applications across social computing, medical informatics, and intelligent agents. Guided by the principles of being Green, Human-Centric, and Trustworthy, we pursue research that is theoretically profound with real-world impact — aiming to build Next-Generation AI for Social Science, empowering AI to truly understand and serve human society.
