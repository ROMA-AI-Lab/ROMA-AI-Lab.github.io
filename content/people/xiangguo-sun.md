Dr. Xiangguo Sun/孙相国 (sun hsiang-kuo) is now working as a Full Professor at Southeast University. He leads **ROMA Lab** (Responsible Open-world Modeling and Agents), an open academic cooperation platform focusing on graph-native intelligence and its applications across social computing, medical informatics, and intelligent agents. Before joining SEU, he worked as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Hong Cheng. He received his Ph.D. in 2022 from Southeast University, supervised by Prof. Bo Liu. In the same year, he visited Zhejiang Lab as a visiting researcher hosted by Chief Scientist Prof. Hongyang Chen. He worked as a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia with Dr. Hang Dong and Bo Qiao. In 2019, he came to Australia as a 2-year joint Ph.D. student at The University of Queensland, supervised by ARC Future Fellow Prof. Hongzhi Yin.

## 🔬 Research Interests

His research interests lie in data mining and graph-native intelligence, with broad applications spanning social computing, medical informatics, and intelligent agents. His work appears in **SIGKDD, VLDB, TKDE, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, WWW, WSDM, CIKM, TOIS, TNNLS, RecSys, IJCAI, EMNLP**. ### Industrial Impact

- His heterogeneous hypergraph model was adopted as a **standard algorithm** in the open-source graph computing library **EasyGraph**, demonstrating both theoretical novelty and community recognition.

- He pioneered the **Universal Graph Prompting** paradigm, achieving performance **surpassing AlphaFold-Multimer** in protein complex structure prediction, showing excellent cross-domain generalization potential.

- He led the development of **ProG**, an open-source graph prompting toolkit widely used as a benchmarking standard, with **560+ GitHub stars**.

- His algorithms serve as **core components** in intelligent public security systems in Nanjing and Jiangsu Province, supporting large-scale criminal investigation and intelligence analysis.

- During the COVID-19 pandemic, his models supported an enterprise-level **grid-based COVID-19 analytics system**, generating substantial social and economic value.

- His multi-behavior recommendation research has been successfully transferred to Tencent and **fully deployed in the WeChat Official Accounts ecosystem**.
